# Project Toothless

Breckenridge Research Group's public record for discoveries, provenance, terminology hygiene, corrections, and provider-contribution history.

Breckenridge publishes its own research findings and synthesis openly; underlying third-party materials retain their original rights and are referenced rather than relicensed where necessary.

## Publication principles

- Findings are public by default after a narrow pre-publication scrub.
- Exclude credentials, secrets, personal/private data, confidential, NDA-covered or beta-restricted material, and third-party content without redistribution rights.
- Use provider codenames in research narrative. Include exact identity only where needed for reproducibility, attribution, licensing, or authorized handback, subject to applicable restrictions.
- Correction is contribution. Publish supported challenges, contrary evidence, and later clarification while preserving the historical path and meaning of prior work.
- Provider contribution does not imply ownership. A request does not create a right. No provider receives another provider's restricted material merely by asking.
- Do not imply a partnership, endorsement, sponsorship, or formal relationship without supporting authorization.
