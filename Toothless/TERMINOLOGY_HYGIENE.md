# Terminology hygiene

Use terms according to their documented role and period. Do not retrofit current architecture or administrative labels onto historical work.

| Term | Meaning in this record | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Grant | Funding instrument or source | May fund a Work Order; is not itself the internal Work Order |
| Internal Work Order | Internal authorization or specification of work | Does not establish an external grant, contract, or provider relationship |
| Houston | Historical/convergence evidence in this research narrative | Not a statement of current architecture or a current provider prescription |
| Provider-native capability convergence | Native services coming to cover some needs previously addressed by bespoke infrastructure | Does not demonstrate universal equivalence or replacement of every custom component |
| Provider contribution | Documented assistance, capability, clarification, or other input | Does not imply ownership, partnership, endorsement, or entitlement to restricted material |
| Correction | Supported revision or clarification of a record | A contribution that preserves the historical path |

Use established codenames such as Maple, Oak, Narni, Birch, and Pine in provider research narrative. No identity mapping is inferred or published in this scaffold. Record exact identity only when needed for reproducibility, attribution, licensing, or authorized handback and permitted for disclosure. If omission prevents reproducibility, state that limitation.

For changed terminology, identify the old usage, period if known, intended historical meaning, current preferred usage, reason, and source. Unknown dates remain unknown. Link the correction to the prior record; do not fabricate the missing prior artifact.
