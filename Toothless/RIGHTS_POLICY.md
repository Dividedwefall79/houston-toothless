# Rights policy

## Publication and reuse

Breckenridge's policy is to publish its own findings and synthesis openly after the narrow scrub. A standard reuse license has not yet been selected or authorized for this scaffold. Public access and an open publication policy do not by themselves grant a general copyright license. No additional reuse permissions are granted by this policy.

Before release, the Breckenridge steward must confirm authority to publish each included item and either retain this explicit rights policy or select a suitable license for material Breckenridge has authority to license. Do not imply that this repository has an established open-source or open-content license while selection is unresolved.

## Third-party material

Third-party materials retain their original rights. Reference them rather than relicensing them. Any permitted inclusion must carry its original license, attribution, and notices as required. A repository license, if later adopted, must explicitly exclude material it cannot cover.

AI assistance or provider contribution does not establish rights clearance, transfer third-party ownership, or entitle a provider to another provider's restricted material. No contributor or provider ownership claim is inferred from participation. A request does not create a right.

Do not publish confidential, NDA-covered, beta-restricted, private, or unlicensed third-party material. Do not assume codenames remove contractual obligations. This policy records the project decision and is not an analysis of specific provider contracts.
