# C-001: Grant and internal Work Order are distinct

## Clarification

A grant is distinct from an internal Work Order. A grant may fund a Work Order; it is not itself the Work Order. Use grant for the funding instrument and internal Work Order for the internal authorization or specification of work.

## Basis and limits

The planning conversation explicitly identifies this distinction as an initial correction. No actual grant instrument, Work Order, award, payment, or external relationship was supplied. This record establishes vocabulary, not the existence or legal classification of a particular funding arrangement.
