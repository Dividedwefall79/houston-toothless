# Provenance

Every substantive record identifies its source, claim type, evidence limits, and correction links. Separate direct observation from author interpretation, conversation-backed synthesis, and independently reproduced results.

The initial backfills use E-001. The planning conversation supports the topics and decisions, but does not supply raw experiments, prior architecture artifacts, provider version information, or original grant/Work Order documents. Do not present it as proof of those missing facts.

## Minimum record

- Stable record ID, title, date recorded, and status.
- Claim and its scope; event date if known, otherwise explicitly unknown.
- Evidence IDs and permitted public references; source author or origin where known.
- Observation versus interpretation, verification performed, uncertainty, and possible contrary evidence.
- Rights basis for any included material and necessary attribution.
- Relevant correction, supersession, and handback references.

Reference third-party sources at their original location rather than copying or relicensing them. A citation does not authorize redistribution. Include third-party content only with documented redistribution rights and required notices. If a restricted source cannot be cited safely, disclose the resulting evidence limitation without revealing the source or its contents.

Use provider codenames in narrative. Include exact identity only where necessary and permitted for reproducibility, attribution, licensing, or authorized handback. Do not infer an identity from a codename or expose a private mapping. Restricted supporting evidence stays outside the public repository, including its Git history.

Provider contribution history records what was contributed, when known, the evidence, and limits. It does not assign ownership or claim endorsement. Preserve original source meaning; later corrections add linked context.
