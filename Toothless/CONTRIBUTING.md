# Contributing

Correction is contribution. Supported challenges, contrary evidence, provider clarification, and reproduced results are welcome.

1. Use the template in findings/, corrections/, or evidence/ and assign an unused stable ID.
2. State the claim, source, uncertainty, and distinction between observation and inference. Do not invent dates, identities, experiments, or contractual rights.
3. Cite public sources and establish redistribution rights before including third-party material. Leave restricted material outside the repository.
4. Apply the narrow scrub in PREPUBLICATION_REVIEW.md to your contribution and any history included with it.
5. Submit for Breckenridge stewardship review through the review channel established at release. No public submission endpoint is configured yet.

Do not submit credentials, personal/private information, confidential, NDA-covered or beta-restricted content, or third-party material lacking redistribution rights. Do not attach restricted evidence merely to substantiate a challenge; offer a permitted reference or describe the verification limitation.

Use provider codenames in narrative, with exact identity only where needed and permitted. Do not imply formal relationships or endorsement. A request for evidence does not create a right to receive it, including another provider's restricted material.

For corrections, identify the affected record and preserve its historical meaning. Describe the change, basis, and remaining uncertainty. If the prior artifact is unavailable, say so.

Contributors retain their applicable rights. Submission does not automatically transfer ownership or grant a license; the steward must establish permission to publish before accepting material. Consult RIGHTS_POLICY.md.
