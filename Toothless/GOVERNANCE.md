# Governance

## Stewardship

Breckenridge Research Group stewards Project Toothless. A designated Breckenridge steward reviews publication, evidence sufficiency, rights, and corrections. No named individual, additional governing body, or provider authority is established by this scaffold. Designate a release steward before the first public release.

The purpose is accountability through traceable observations, interpretations, corrections, and authorized handback. Provider participation or assistance does not convey ownership or control of the repository. Rights arise from applicable agreements and law, not contribution labels or requests.

## Decisions and corrections

Contributors propose records using the templates. The steward checks source traceability, claim scope, exclusions, and terminology. Record material decisions with date, rationale, evidence references, and the affected record IDs. Unresolved disagreements remain visible as disagreements; do not convert them into established findings.

Correction is contribution. Link a correction from the affected record, describe what was meant at the time, and distinguish the revised interpretation. Preserve historical meaning rather than silently rewriting prior work. Fix incidental spelling and broken links without changing substance. If unsafe material appears, remove it promptly; preservation never requires continued exposure of secrets or restricted data. Assess Git-history remediation and affected distribution separately.

## Publication and handback

Findings are public by default after the narrow scrub in PREPUBLICATION_REVIEW.md. Publish Breckenridge-authored findings and synthesis openly, with rights boundaries intact. Exact provider identity is used only when necessary and permitted; codenames are not a substitute for checking restrictions.

No provider receives another provider's restricted material merely by asking. A request does not create a right. Before handback, establish the recipient's entitlement and authority to receive each item. Share cleared Breckenridge synthesis or permitted references where appropriate, and omit restricted underlying material. Do not commit restricted handback packages or private entitlement records to this repository.

## Responsibility and limits

The steward is responsible for release decisions and correction handling. Records distinguish observation, inference, and unresolved claims. The repository makes no claim that contractual restrictions have been waived, that rights have been legally cleared by an AI assistant, or that a provider endorses its conclusions. This policy does not itself resolve liability or alter third-party agreements.

## First release

1. Assign a release steward and confirm the rights policy or choose an authorized license.
2. Inspect the proposed files and any Git history for excluded material and unsupported relationship claims.
3. Record scope, outcome, unresolved items, and the exact release revision.
4. Obtain the user's explicit approval of the destination and final release before pushing or publishing.

Subsequent releases receive the same narrow content scrub and a traceable review record.
